## About fOrge

**fOrge** is an African venture-building institution and innovation studio dedicated to transforming important problems into scalable systems, technologies, businesses, and infrastructure. 

We do not exist to build just one product; we exist to **repeatedly build successful ventures**.

> *"We do not build projects. We build institutions."* — **The fOrge Creed**

---

## Our Mission

fOrge exists to systematically execute the venture lifecycle:
* Identify real, high-impact problems.
* Mobilize multidisciplinary expertise and research.
* Design practical technical and architectural solutions.
* Secure resources, partnerships, and funding.
* Validate, commercialize, and spin out sustainable companies.
* Retain strategic institutional value for future growth.

---

## 📐 The Core Philosophy & Rules

Our operations are governed by immutable principles that prioritize systemic execution over short-term trends:
1. **Problems before technology.**
2. **Evidence before assumptions.**
3. **Execution before publicity.**
4. **Funding before unnecessary spending.**
5. **Systems before personalities.**
6. **Long-term value over short-term wins.**
7. **Build institutions, not projects.**

---

## Intellectual Property & Governance

All source code, models, architecture, designs, research, databases, and digital infrastructure developed under fOrge remain strictly protected under institutional governance frameworks. 

* **Infrastructure Control:** All repositories, domains, cloud infrastructure, and deployments remain under authorized founder stewardship.
* **Access Model:** Built on strict need-to-know access and role-based permissions.
