## About fOrge

**fOrge** is an African venture-building institution and innovation studio dedicated to transforming important problems into scalable systems, technologies, businesses, and infrastructure[cite: 2]. 

We do not exist to build just one product; we exist to **repeatedly build successful ventures**[cite: 2].

> *"We do not build projects. We build institutions."* — **The fOrge Creed**[cite: 2]

---

## Our Mission

fOrge exists to systematically execute the venture lifecycle[cite: 2]:
* Identify real, high-impact problems[cite: 2].
* Mobilize multidisciplinary expertise and research[cite: 2].
* Design practical technical and architectural solutions[cite: 2].
* Secure resources, partnerships, and funding[cite: 2].
* Validate, commercialize, and spin out sustainable companies[cite: 2].
* Retain strategic institutional value for future growth[cite: 2].

---

## 📐 The Core Philosophy & Rules

Our operations are governed by immutable principles that prioritize systemic execution over short-term trends[cite: 2]:
1. **Problems before technology.**[cite: 2]
2. **Evidence before assumptions.**[cite: 2]
3. **Execution before publicity.**[cite: 2]
4. **Funding before unnecessary spending.**[cite: 2]
5. **Systems before personalities.**[cite: 2]
6. **Long-term value over short-term wins.**[cite: 2]
7. **Build institutions, not projects.**[cite: 2]

## Intellectual Property & Governance

All source code, models, architecture, designs, research, databases, and digital infrastructure developed under fOrge remain strictly protected under institutional governance frameworks[cite: 2]. 

* **Infrastructure Control:** All repositories, domains, cloud infrastructure, and deployments remain under authorized founder stewardship[cite: 2].
* **Access Model:** Built on strict need-to-know access and role-based permissions[cite: 2].
