## About fOrge

fOrge is an African venture-building institution and innovation studio. We transform important problems into scalable systems, technologies, businesses and infrastructure, designed for the realities of the people who use them.

We do not exist to build just one product. We exist to build successful ventures, repeatedly.

> *"We do not build projects. We build institutions."*
> — The fOrge Creed

## Our Mission

fOrge systematically executes the full venture lifecycle:

1. **Identify** real, high-impact problems.
2. **Mobilize** multidisciplinary expertise and research.
3. **Design** practical technical and architectural solutions.
4. **Secure** resources, partnerships and funding.
5. **Validate, commercialize and spin out** sustainable companies.
6. **Retain** strategic institutional value for future growth.

## Core Principles

Our operations are governed by principles that put systemic execution ahead of short-term trends:

1. Problems before technology.
2. Evidence before assumptions.
3. Execution before publicity.
4. Funding before unnecessary spending.
5. Systems before personalities.
6. Long-term value over short-term wins.
7. Build institutions, not projects.

## How We Build

Our solutions are designed for African operating realities, not imported assumptions:

- **Mobile-first**, for the phone in our users' hands.
- **Low-bandwidth and resilient**, working when connectivity is slow or unreliable.
- **French-first** where it serves our users.
- **Local payment realities** built in from the start.
- **Affordable** for the businesses we serve.
- **Secure by default**, with strong fraud and security controls.
- **Modular**, so each solution can scale from Burkina Faso across West Africa.

## Intellectual Property & Governance

All source code, models, architecture, designs, research, databases and digital infrastructure developed under fOrge belong to fOrge and are protected under institutional governance frameworks.

- **Institutional control:** repositories, domains, cloud infrastructure and deployments are owned and controlled by fOrge as an institution, never by any single individual.
- **Access model:** strict need-to-know access and role-based permissions.
- **Continuity by design:** code, decisions and documentation are recorded in fOrge's systems, so every venture outlives any individual contributor.
