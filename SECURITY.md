# Security Policy

fOrge builds commerce and business infrastructure that handles money, stock and customer information. We take the security of our products, and of the people who use them, seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities in GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions or chat groups.**

Email **`<security@your-domain>`** with:

- a description of the vulnerability and its potential impact;
- the affected product (for example Kosse+) and, if known, the version or URL;
- clear steps to reproduce it;
- your name and how to contact you (optional).

## What to expect

- **Acknowledgement** within 3 business days.
- **Initial assessment** within 10 business days, including whether we confirm the issue.
- **Updates** as we work on a fix, and notification when it is resolved.
- **Credit** for your discovery once fixed, if you wish.

## Scope

In scope: all fOrge products and services, including Kosse+.

Out of scope: social engineering of fOrge staff or users, physical attacks, denial-of-service testing, and spam.

## Good-faith research

We will not pursue action against researchers who act in good faith, which means you:

- do not access, change or delete data belonging to other users;
- do not disrupt or degrade our services;
- do not use or share the vulnerability before we have had reasonable time to fix it;
- report what you find to us promptly and only to us.

## For fOrge team members

If you discover a vulnerability or suspect a leak (including a secret committed by accident), report it immediately to the Platform-Admin team at the address above. Do not describe the vulnerability in issues, commit messages or team chats until it is fixed. Reporting quickly is always the right decision.
