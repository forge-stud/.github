# Code of Conduct

Everyone who works on fOrge, whether employee, contractor or ambassador, agrees to:

- **Be respectful.** Treat colleagues, users and partners with courtesy, whatever their role, background or experience.
- **Review the work, not the person.** Feedback on code and documents is specific, constructive and kind.
- **Be honest.** Report mistakes, risks and delays early. Problems raised early are cheap; problems hidden are expensive.
- **Protect confidentiality.** fOrge code, customer data and internal discussions stay inside fOrge.
- **Act in fOrge's interest.** Avoid conflicts of interest and declare any that arise.

Harassment, discrimination and intimidation are not tolerated.

To report a concern, contact the Chairman or the Platform-Admin team privately. Reports are handled confidentially.
